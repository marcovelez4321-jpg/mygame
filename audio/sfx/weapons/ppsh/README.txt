PPSH
FORMAT: WAV (or OGG), 44.1 or 48 kHz. Gunshots you fire yourself play
non-positional, so stereo is fine here.
No silence at the start, short fade at the end, peaks around -1 dBFS.
Number each variation: fire_01.wav, fire_02.wav, ... -- one plays at random per shot,
so 3-5 slightly different shots keep full-auto from sounding like a machine gun loop.

LIVE:
fire_01 .. 05           one PPSh shot. Until there's at least one file here, the PPSh
                        uses the Uzi's (machine_gun) fire sound.
