DOORS, BUTTONS, LEVERS AND KEYS
FORMAT: WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 24-bit, MONO (sounds in the 3D world must be mono).
No silence at the start, short fade at the end, peaks around -1 dBFS.
Number each variation: name_01.wav, name_02.wav, ... (one is fine to start, more variations sound better)

LIVE:
door_move_01 .. 03          a door starting to slide/swing (stone grind, metal rumble, wooden creak)
door_stop_01 .. 03          the door hitting fully open or shut (thud, clang)
door_locked_01 .. 02        trying a locked door (rattle, denied clunk)
button_press_01 .. 03       pressing a button in (click, chunky switch)
lever_pull_01 .. 03         pulling a lever (ratchet, heavy metal lever)
key_pickup_01               grabbing a key (jingle)
