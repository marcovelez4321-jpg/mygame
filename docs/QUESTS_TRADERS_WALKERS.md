# Quests, traders, walkers and angels (the user's, 2026-10-25)

Every detail here is the user's call (Rule 10). Still open: marked **(to ask)**.

## Build order
1. **Quest facts + world changes** (the backbone).
2. Traders and scrap.
3. The walkers (the Pilgrim, the Crawling Ossuary).

## Lore
- **The Stalker is a myth** that has ascended to godlike status, from how many
  it has killed and how many bloodlines it has ended. Traders half-believe in
  it.
- **An ending** is reached through a ritual. Its ingredients so far: the
  Seraph's feather, and the Bounty's reward. **(More to come.)**
- The cult: **a new faction of hooded humans** (existing human models in robes
  and hoods) with their own camps. They attack you on sight unless you've
  sided with them (the Bounty).

## Quest facts and world changes
- One shared record of world facts on the host, saved and mirrored to every
  player. Quests write facts (what you did, how, what you killed or spared)
  and read them (lines, rewards, outcomes).
- World changes are editor-placed nodes tied to facts (show, hide, swap).
- **Co-op: quests are shared.** One web for the group: whoever does it, the
  world changes for everyone. Trader consequences stay per player.
- Dialogue with choices uses Grandma's dialogue system.

### The journal
- **A notebook item** in the backpack; double-click to read it.
- **Notes and sketches:** scrawled notes plus small drawings (the torn
  banner, the bell, the antenna).
- **Done quests keep a line** on how you finished them ("Smoked them. Grandma
  won't look at me.").
- **Co-op: own notes.** The quests are shared, but each player's journal
  words them its own way (e.g. who did it).

## The quests (first web)
Givers: Grandma, traders, new NPCs, notes and the camp radio.

1. **The Last Pack** (Grandma).
   - **Finding them:** the cigarettes lie in the Stack in a bright open spot,
     clearly visible once you're close.
   - **The snatch:** as you approach to grab them, whatever your speed, the
     Stalker scrambles past you from behind (as if it had been standing right
     there), snatches the pack in front of you and scrambles out of sight,
     back to its lair with it.
   - **The lair:** the flooded depths. **It's home, watching.** Grab the pack
     and it comes for you: a chase out of the depths.
   - **Then:**
     - **smoke them:** three pods ripen on the Flesh Tree at once (like
       reaching 14 points);
     - **sell them** to a trader: 50 scrap;
     - **give them to Grandma.**
2. **The Bell** (two climbers at 400 m: **a priest** who worships the angels
   and **a heretic** who wants them gone). A great bronze bell on a terrace at
   **1 km**. Pick a side; each pays **30 scrap**.
   - **Ring it:** Seraphim from **500 m** (down from 1 km), for good.
   - **Silence it** (cut it down): Seraphim only from **2 km**.
3. **Bones for the Voice** (a trader). He wants proof of the lair, to prove
   the Stalker exists. **Reward: 40 scrap.** You always bring bones as the
   proof.
   - **The offering:** a bound bundle of meat and teeth, **10 scrap**, rare in
     traders' stock. He mumbles that an offering might put it at ease.
   - **No offering:** its grudge on you is maxed (it picks you first) and it's
     furious for 10 minutes, starting as you leave the lair.
   - **Offering left:** it leaves a gift. The next time you camp, there's loot
     by your bed instead of the usual signs.
4. **Feed the Tree** (a cultist at a bone shrine out in the wilds). Bring a big
   one's raw meat to the Flesh Tree. **Reward: 7 scrap and a revolver.**
   - **Fed:** two more draping roots over Grandma's house (4 in all), and the
     red weed thickens.
   - **Refused:** cult ambushes now and then.
5. **The Climber's Tags** (a note on a body somewhere in the Stack; it floats
   and spins like the keys). It leads to his camp at **1 km**: a light gun
   plus supplies.
   - **Return his tags** to his sister, who **travels with a wandering trader**.
     Her later quest is "Climb for him": reach the top of a tower and leave
     his tags there.
   - Or keep and sell his gear.
6. **The Antenna** (the radio). The mast is on the **1.4 km tower's top**.
   - **Repair it: 25 scrap and 8 silk.** The camp radio then plays hints
     (where wanderers are heading, where the cigarettes, feather or Fanatic
     might be), new quests, warnings (Stalker sightings, storms, the walkers'
     routes) and music at night (the user's tracks).
   - **Sabotage it** for the shrine cultist: **30 scrap**, and the radio goes
     dead.
7. **The Bounty** (a new NPC, secretly a cultist). He wants one of the
   wandering traders dead: the one whose **banner is torn across the eye**.
   "Do this for me and anything you've ever wanted can come true."
   - **Kill the right trader:** you're sided with the cultists (they no
     longer attack you on sight) and get **a ritual ingredient**.
   - **Kill the wrong one:** the usual trader consequences, the cult mocks
     you, and the quest is failed for good.
   - **Warn the target:** a permanent **25% discount** from him, and the cult
     marks you.
   - **Kill the bounty man:** his body has a cult note leading to the Feed the
     Tree cultist, who now refuses you.
   - **Refuse:** nothing for now.
8. **The Feather** (a note). Bring back a Seraph's feather from 1 km.
   - **Give it to a trader:** 100 scrap and a permanent 25% discount from him.
   - **Keep it:** one ingredient for the ritual that leads to an ending.
9. **The Fanatic** (found at random climbing the Stacks). He's hauling one
   huge improvised bomb up the main tower, piece by piece.
   - **Help him:** 40 scrap and 10 grenades. The top of the **1.4 km tower**
     collapses: the skyline changes.
   - **Talk him down** (Grandma-style dialogue): he sells explosives and turns
     up in a later quest.
   - **Too aggressive in the dialogue, or attack him:** he blows himself up
     in an immersive kamikaze animation, **25 m, 300 damage**.

Not in: the Trader's Son, the Nest on the Road, Grandma's First Key, Silence
the Heart.

### Links between them
- **Offering first, no chase:** leave the offering in the lair before taking
  the cigarettes, and it lets you go.
- **Grandma smells it:** smoke the pack and her lines turn cold; her next
  quest pays **nothing**.
- **The bell brings the feather down:** ring it and the Seraphim are at 500 m,
  so the feather is easier to reach, but the Stack is more dangerous.
- **The bomb takes the antenna:** help the Fanatic and the radio dies for good,
  fixed or not.
- **The sister follows the torn banner:** she travels with the bounty's
  target. Kill him and she's alone, hates you, and refuses her quest.
- **The radio finds the Fanatic:** a fixed antenna tells you where he was last
  seen and how far his bomb has got.
- **Fed tree, trusted:** feed the tree and the bounty man drops hints about
  the ritual.
- **The warned trader pays for the feather:** warn the torn-banner trader and
  he pays double for it (200 scrap).

## The Stack's high interiors
Six hand-placed areas, starting where the halls end (400 m). Each is its own
mix of sacred concrete chapel and machinery. More dangerous than the halls:
traps of the place, and new enemies.

1. **The Turbine Nave (~500 m):** a giant chapel whose pews are rows of dead
   turbines; the altar is a generator. Trap: blades that spin back up when
   you trip the power.
2. **The Winding Cathedral (~900 m):** the lift's winding-gear hall, with
   cable drums hung like bells and grated floors over the open shaft. Trap:
   grates that give way.
3. **The Choir of Pipes (~1.5 km):** plumbing grown into organ pipes up every
   wall, singing in the wind. Trap: steam vents blasting across the
   walkways.
4. **The Reliquary Foundry (~2.2 km):** hooks on a moving line cross a chasm
   like pilgrims; crucibles serve as fonts. Trap: ride the hooks or fall;
   the line stops at random.
5. **The Clock of Hours (~3 km):** a vast bell-clock whose turning gears are
   the floors, with the hour bell tolling. Trap: gears that crush or throw
   you on the hour.
6. **The Last Nave (~4.2 km):** pure sacred chapel, with slit windows of angel
   light and a giant's altar. Trap: the floor collapses section by section
   into open sky.

### New enemies (only up there)
- **The Consumed:** a human fully consumed by the virus, grown roach-like bug
  wings.
  - **Look and motion:** its arms and legs dangle with jiggly, almost dead
    physics, while its head stays up and aims perfectly at you, with the
    Stalker's eyes.
  - **Attack:** it rushes and flings itself at you with no regard for itself,
    reacting to everything with physics. It's there to be annoying and push
    you around. Each hit is **15 damage and a 12 m/s shove**; it bounces off,
    tumbles, recovers and comes again.
  - **Stats:** **40 hp, 22 m/s.**
  - **Where:** the high interiors and outside above 400 m, 2-4 at a time.
- **Bell-wraiths:** corroded bells whose clappers are long grasping arms.
  - **Trigger:** only the place's own bells and machines wake them (traps
    start them); guns don't. Woken, they swing down at you.
  - **Stats:** **80 hp, 20 a grab.**

## Scrap and traders
- **Currency: scrap** for now, **per player** in co-op.
- **Buying:** open the backpack, double-click your scrap (into your right
  hand), press F on an item: the price comes off all your scrap (hand and
  bag), and the item goes into your right hand. Items can't be grappled off.
- **Prices (scrap):**
  - guns: light (Pistol, Revolver, MP5) 8; SMGs (Submachine Gun, PPSh, Dual
    MP5s) 15; heavy (Shotgun, Sniper, PKM) 25; RPG 40;
  - ammo per buy: bullets 30 for 1; shells 8 for 2; 1 rocket for 4; 1 frag
    grenade for 3;
  - pills 3, bandage 2, cooked meat 2, camp kit 6;
  - the offering 10 (rare).
- **Stock:** any item can turn up; 12 random items, rarer the pricier;
  re-rolled at every new meeting.
- **Three wandering traders** walk between landmarks, resting at each.
- **Parked traders:** one per landmark, plus three up the Stack at 400 m,
  2 km and 4 km. The high ones are rolled among skybridges, block-top
  terraces and grown-on secret rooms, with a 10% chance of a window room.
  They have the same stock rules, the same prices at every height, and the
  same defence.
- **Hurting a trader** counts as robbing him. He calls for help (8 armed
  humans; the bell isn't built yet), runs, then ambushes you from cover:
  **500 hp, PKM.**
- **Consequences** (per player, never fading):
  - every trader charges +100%;
  - half of them refuse you;
  - his cart stays as a wreck (a landmark);
  - a replacement comes after 4 in-game days.

### The rickshaw shop (wanderers pull it; parked ones too)
- Japanese jinrikisha shape, **oversized: 3 m long, 2.5 m tall**.
- **Overgrown** with coral (staghorn and brain coral on the wheels and frame,
  glowing tips), glow moss, and kelp trailing with glowing bulbs.
- Two big spoked wood wheels; worn **dark red lacquer**; a small **wooden
  roof** with curved eaves.
- The seat is **his shop counter**, with a hanging scale, a small oil lamp
  and a bell.
- A hanging **deep red cloth banner** with a painted **eye**.
- Light: **paper lanterns and the growth's glow** both.
- **Display, always shown (even on the move):** guns and grenades on hooks
  along the sides; ammo, food, meds and camp kits on fold-down side shelves;
  small items hanging from the roof's eaves.
- **The trader:** an old man bent from pulling, in a straw kasa hat; he
  **mumbles, no words** (prices on the tags); model: a human from the enemy
  set.

## Angels (biblically accurate)
- **Seraphim** (six wings) from **1 km** up (500 m or 2 km after the Bell);
  **Ophanim** (eyed, burning wheels within wheels) from **3 km** up.
- At the Stack's heights and on both walkers: the higher you are, the closer
  they are.
- **Wardens:** they guard the heights. Looking at a Seraph hurts, getting
  close is dangerous, and Ophanim block routes until a quest or item lets
  you pass.

## The walkers (moving megastructures)
- **All bone and flesh** (the buildings on them are the only concrete).
- **The Pilgrim:** a colossal hunched giant carrying a **brutalist cathedral**
  on its back (gothic proportions in raw concrete). **6 km tall.** 2 m/s.
- **The Crawling Ossuary:** a castle on a low, spider-like body of bone
  arches, carrying a **bone cathedral-crypt** (charnel church, skull-lined
  crypts, bone spires). **1.5 km long, 500 m tall.** 6 m/s.
- **Boarding** (all four): rest stops, grappling on mid-walk, climbing a leg
  as it plants, or jumping on from a high place.
- **(To ask when built:)** their routes, rest stops, the insides, what lives
  on them, footfall danger.
- **How (technical):**
  - a slow-moving body with few merged collision shapes that carries what
    stands on it;
  - the host syncs one number (how far along its route it is);
  - shell, interior and decor by distance;
  - legs step onto the real terrain.
