# Quests, traders, walkers and angels (the user's, 2026-10-25)

Every detail here is the user's call (Rule 10). Still open: marked **(to ask)**.

## Build order
1. **Quest facts + world changes** (the backbone).
2. Traders and scrap.
3. The walkers (the Pilgrim, the Crawling Ossuary).

## Lore
- **The Stalker is a myth** that has ascended to godlike status, from how many
  it has killed and how many bloodlines it has ended. Traders half-believe in
  it.
- **An ending** is reached through a ritual. Its ingredients so far: the
  Seraph's feather, and the Bounty's reward. **(More to come.)**
- The cult: **a new faction of hooded humans** (existing human models in robes
  and hoods) with their own camps. They attack you on sight unless you've
  sided with them (the Bounty).

## Quest facts and world changes
- One shared record of world facts on the host, saved and mirrored to every
  player. Quests write facts (what you did, how, what you killed or spared)
  and read them (lines, rewards, outcomes).
- World changes are editor-placed nodes tied to facts (show, hide, swap).
- **Co-op: quests are shared.** One web for the group: whoever does it, the
  world changes for everyone. Trader consequences stay per player.
- Dialogue with choices uses Grandma's dialogue system.

### The journal
- **A notebook item** in the backpack; double-click to read it.
- **Notes and sketches:** scrawled notes plus small drawings (the torn
  banner, the bell, the antenna).
- **Done quests keep a line** on how you finished them ("Smoked them. Grandma
  won't look at me.").
- **Co-op: own notes.** The quests are shared, but each player's journal
  words them its own way (e.g. who did it).

## The Stack's high interiors
Six hand-placed areas, starting where the halls end (400 m). Each is its own
mix of sacred concrete chapel and machinery. More dangerous than the halls:
traps of the place, and new enemies.

1. **The Turbine Nave (~500 m):** a giant chapel whose pews are rows of dead
   turbines; the altar is a generator. Trap: blades that spin back up when
   you trip the power.
2. **The Winding Cathedral (~900 m):** the lift's winding-gear hall, with
   cable drums hung like bells and grated floors over the open shaft. Trap:
   grates that give way.
3. **The Choir of Pipes (~1.5 km):** plumbing grown into organ pipes up every
   wall, singing in the wind. Trap: steam vents blasting across the
   walkways.
4. **The Reliquary Foundry (~2.2 km):** hooks on a moving line cross a chasm
   like pilgrims; crucibles serve as fonts. Trap: ride the hooks or fall;
   the line stops at random.
5. **The Clock of Hours (~3 km):** a vast bell-clock whose turning gears are
   the floors, with the hour bell tolling. Trap: gears that crush or throw
   you on the hour.
6. **The Last Nave (~4.2 km):** pure sacred chapel, with slit windows of angel
   light and a giant's altar. Trap: the floor collapses section by section
   into open sky.

  hand), press F on an item: the price comes off all your scrap (hand and
  bag), and the item goes into your right hand. Items can't be grappled off.

## Built: the backbone (2026-10-25)

### World facts
- **What it is:** `WorldFacts` (`scripts/quests/world_facts.gd`), held in
  `Network.facts`.
- **Who owns it:** the host. Facts are saved to `user://world_facts.cfg`,
  mirrored to every game, and sent whole to late joiners. A client holds the
  host's facts while it's in that game, and gets its own back when it leaves.
- **Writing:** `Network.send_fact(name, value)`, host or offline only.
  Saving is automatic.
- **Reading:** `WorldFacts.get_fact(name, default)`.
- **Listening:** `Network.fact_changed(name, value)`.
- **Naming:** `quest.<id>` holds the state (0 not started, 1 open, 2 done).
  Each quest also has `.hint`, `.outcome`, `.by` and `.by_name`.
- **Grandma's cigarettes quest now lives in facts.** It survives quitting.
- **Testing:** F9 → "Forget the world (quest facts)" wipes every fact.

### World changes
- **The node:** `WorldChange` (`scripts/quests/world_change.gd`). Put scenery
  under it and set `fact`, `value` and `present_when_matching`.
  - `value` is text: `"true"`, `"2"`, `"smoked"`. Leave it empty to mean
    "the fact is set at all".
  - `present_when_matching` on: the children are there while the fact
    matches. Off: they're gone while it matches.
- **Swapping:** for something that turns into something else, use two
  WorldChanges on the same fact with opposite `present_when_matching`.
- **Cost:** children that aren't there are taken out of the tree, so nothing
  is drawn and there's no collision.
- **Timing:** changes happen at once, on every game.
- **Limit:** scenery only, no enemies or other networked things.

### The journal
- **The item:** `Backpack.Kind.JOURNAL`, shown as the PSX notebook. You start
  with it and never lose it: it's never dropped or left on death, and it
  takes no room or weight.
- **Reading it:** double-click it in the bag, then close the bag. It comes up
  open (`JournalView`), half the screen wide, lower middle, square to your
  view, drawn above the PS1 dither (canvas layer 7), the pages rendered at 2x
  in a thickened scrawl.
  - Left click turns forward a spread, right click turns back (the grapple
    is off meanwhile).
  - Only F puts it away; picking anything up (walked into, grappled, a death
    pack) does too. G does nothing. You can move as normal while reading.
- **The pages** (`JournalPage`): grey plain paper, grubby fingerprints,
  pencil grey ink, the Reenie Beanie scrawl, and code-drawn pencil sketches.
- **The entries** (`QuestJournal.ENTRIES`):
  - one page per quest, in the user's words;
  - a done quest has its open note crossed out, with how it ended
    underneath;
  - own notes in co-op: "I" when it was you, a teammate's name when it was
    them ("Someone" with no Steam).

## Built: The Last Pack (2026-10-25)

### The quest's steps
`LastPack` (`scripts/quests/last_pack.gd`) keeps them in the fact
`quest.cigarettes.stage`, so they survive quitting:
- **"" (lying out):** the pack lies in the Stack (StackSecrets) as a bait
  CarryItem.
  - It sits in a shaft of light from above: a spotlight, a faint beam and

  - It can't be walked into, grabbed or grappled.
  - Each game builds the light itself; it stays lit, and empty, after the

- **"lair":** it's on its biggest bone pile in the lair (StalkerLair's
  trophy pile).
- **"recovered":** someone took it back out. Quit before handing it in or
  smoking it and it's home again next session (`LastPack.new_session()`).

### The snatch (`Stalker._pack_tick`, `_snatch_pack`, `_pack_snatch_tick`)
- **The trigger:** a player comes within 12 m of the bait. It happens
  whatever the Stalker is doing (even away or mid-stunt), and fire doesn't
  stop it.
- **The scramble:** it's placed 4 m behind them, then scrambles past at
  25 m/s (its lunge), 1.5 m to their side, skittering.
- **The grab:** it takes the pack in its hand and shrieks (the new
  `pack_shriek` slot, falling back to the made-up shriek).
- **Gone:** it runs away from everyone until it's been unseen for 1 s. Then
  it's home, and the pack is in the lair.
- **Counts as** a big moment for the director.

### The lair
- **Before you take it:** while the pack is on its bones, it's home whenever
  anyone comes within 120 m. It only watches: no warning, no defence lunge,
  and it doesn't leave while anyone's within 150 m.
- **Taking it starts the chase:** 60 s at its sprint (22 m/s) straight at
  the carrier.
  - Within grab range it lunges: the usual grab, and you keep the pack.
  - One strike ends the chase. Newcomers aren't spared.
  - The chase-start sound is the `starts_hunting` slot.

### Smoking it
- **How:** pack in hand, left click. You stand still for 6 s (quick and
  quiet), with one puff of smoke halfway through (the reused backblast
  puff).
- **Then:** you get a mutation's worth of points (14), so three pods ripen
  on the Flesh Tree: a free mutation.
- **What it records:**
  - `quest.cigarettes.outcome` = "smoked", and the quest is done;
  - `grandma.next_unpaid` = true. Her next quest checks
    `GrandmaQuests.pays()` and calls `paid()`.
- **Grandma's greeting** from then on: "You smell like my cigarettes."
  (`GrandmaDialogue.smoked_greeting`).

## Built: the Crawling Ossuary, phase 1 -- the walking body (2026-10-25)

### The building and its legs (redesigned)
- **The building:** baked from `maps/megastructures/the_ossuary.map` into

    hyperplane_size 2048), or re-run the tool, which overwrites your map.
- **The legs** (OssuaryBones): three bones each.
  - The femur is 430 m long, the tibia 380 m, and the sharp shin 150 m;
    their radii are 20, 17 and 15 m.
  - The hips are at the building's corners (100, 12, 195); the feet go out
    to (470, 420).
### How it walks (Ossuary)
Everything comes from one number, its time, plus its route's seed.
- **Its cycle:** walk 600 s at 6 m/s, settle 20 s, rest 180 s, rise 20 s.
- **Resting:** the underside goes from 150 m down to the ground.
- **The gait:** 150 m strides in diagonal pairs (front-left with back-right,
  then the others). Each foot lands out at its corner's stance under
  where its hip will be mid-stride, on the terrain. It swings for half the
  stride, lifted 40 m, and is planted the other half. The knees are two-bone
  IK (up and out).
- **Its route:** it wanders at random, waypoints 1.2-2.5 km apart within
  3.2 km of the world's middle.
  - It steers in 20 m steps, turning at most 0.05 rad a step.
  - It keeps 1.5 km from the Stack and 1 km from Grandma's house. (My
    choice: so its legs never walk through them.)
- **Its tilt:** pitched at most 8 degrees with the land, and level side to
  side.
- **Riding it:** the building and every leg bone are moving platforms
  (AnimatableBody3D).

### Footfalls
- **Boom:** heard to 1 km.
- **Crater:** a decal 36 m across that fades over its last 30 s and is gone
  at 10 min.
- **Dust:** a burst where the foot lands.
- **Shake:** your camera shakes within 80 m.
- **The host's:**
  - players under a foot (within 15 m, not far above it) die;
  - players within 80 m and on the ground are shoved 10 m/s out and 6 m/s
    up (my numbers);
  - creatures under a foot die.

### Sound (WalkerSound)
- **Slots:** drop files named `footfall_NN`, `creak_NN`, `bell_NN` or
  `choir_NN` into `audio/sfx/walkers/`. Until then, code-made placeholders.
- **Creaks:** at a random knee every 3-7 s, unless it's resting. Heard to
  350 m.
- **Bell:** as it settles and as it rises, heard to 1.5 km.
- **Choir:** looped in the chapel, heard to 220 m.

### Co-op
- The host sends its seed, time and start spot every 0.5 s.
- Clients build the walker on the first one they hear and ease their time
  toward the host's, snapping back if more than 5 s off.
