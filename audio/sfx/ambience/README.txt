AMBIENCE -- LIVE (plays in every level, kept very subtle)
FORMAT: WAV or OGG, 44.1 or 48 kHz. Beds can be STEREO; one-shots MONO.

oneshot_anylevel_01 .. 12+  random distant details (creaks, wind, far-off noises). One plays
							every 8-25 s from a random direction 8-18 m around you, at -16 dB.
bed_anylevel_loop_01        optional LOOP: a constant background atmosphere under everything,
							at -22 dB. Seamless, cut on zero crossings.

Volume of the whole category: pause menu > Audio > Ambience.
Per-sound volume/pitch: audio/events/ambience/*.tres. Timing and distance: the AmbiencePlayer
script (scripts/ambience_player.gd) exports.
