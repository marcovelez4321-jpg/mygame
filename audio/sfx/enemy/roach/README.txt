FLYING ROACH
FORMAT: WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz, 24-bit, MONO (sounds in the 3D world must be mono).
No silence at the start, short fade at the end, peaks around -1 dBFS.
Loops: seamless, cut on zero crossings.
Number each variation: name_01.wav, name_02.wav, ...

LIVE:
buzz_loop_01            LOOP: wings buzzing. The game raises its pitch as the roach gets closer and faster
						(like HL2's manhack), so record it at a lowish, natural pitch.
dive_01 .. 03           a short angry screech/chitter as it starts a dive at you
bite_01 .. 04           it connects: a crunchy bite/slice
death_01 .. 04          shot dead: a wet crunch/pop
splat_01 .. 04          a dead roach hitting the ground (wet splat, a squish)
spit_01 .. 03           SPITTER: hocking a glob of acid (a wet hork/ptoo)
splash_01 .. 04         SPITTER: the glob landing (wet splat with a little sizzle)

Foley ideas: an electric toothbrush or hair clipper against paper (buzz), crunching
a crisp or snapping celery (bite), squashing a grape or wet bread (death).
