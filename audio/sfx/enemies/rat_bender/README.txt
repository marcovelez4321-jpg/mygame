Rat Bender and rat horde sounds. Drop .wav or .ogg files in this folder named:
  scurry_01.wav ...   a LOOP of many little feet -- plays once for the whole pack, at its middle
  bite_01.wav ...     a rat biting (squeak + snap)
  cast_01.wav ...     the Rat Bender winding up a spell (rush and leap)
  summon_01.wav ...   the Rat Bender summoning more rats
  heal_01.wav ...     the Rat Bender channeling his heal
Several files with the same start (bite_01, bite_02) = one is picked at random each time.
